核心方法与能力边界 Method Overview & Failure Boundary

01

示范时序修复与世界模型特征对齐Timestamp Alignment & World-Model Supervision

通过 MuJoCo 快照回放修复示范时序错位，按 3:1 混合采样新旧任务；在 PaliGemma 末 10 层注入 LoRA 并解冻动作专家末 6 层，引入第 t+9 帧视觉潜特征与潜动作对齐损失完成微调适配。 Fixes post-action demonstration offsets via MuJoCo snapshot replay with 3:1 target-source mixing; injects LoRA into the last 10 PaliGemma layers and unfreezes the last 6 action expert layers under future t+9 visual-latent and latent-action alignment losses.

02

多初噪闭环采样与拒绝采样微调（RFT）Closed-Loop Rollout Mining & RFT

针对开环行为克隆在接触操作中易滑脱的问题，基于多组初始噪声采集 200 条闭环轨迹；对比分析 Flow-DPO 在连续动作空间偏离专家分布的原因（Dev 42.0%），采用拒绝采样微调筛选高质量动作块（Dev 82.0%）。 Collects 200 multi-seed closed-loop rollouts to address contact slip in open-loop behavioral cloning; diagnoses why continuous-space Flow-DPO drifts off narrow expert manifolds (42.0% Dev) and adopts Rejection Fine-Tuning (82.0% Dev).

03

单底座 LoRA 语义路由Single-Backbone LoRA Semantic Routing

以单份常驻主干替代双模型硬路由，将推理显存由 17.8 GiB 压降至 9.9 GiB；基于语言潜空间余弦原型门控原位切换适配器，在 50 条同义改写指令与 40 条原任务指令上均取得 100% 路由准确率。 Replaces dual-model routing with one resident backbone, cutting VRAM from 17.8 GiB to 9.9 GiB; in-place LoRA switching via latent cosine prototype gating achieves 100% recall on 50 zero-shot paraphrases and 100% specificity on source tasks.

04

两步 Rectified Flow 蒸馏与推理加速Two-Step Rectified Flow & Inference Acceleration

结合 torch.compile 与 Rectified Flow 将流去噪由未编译 10 步蒸馏压缩至 2 步（去噪耗时 258.8 ms → 9.7 ms，整块动作推理 415.9 ms → 157.3 ms）；步长自适应 RTC 前缀引导（|Δt|·w ≤ 0.22）相比无引导少步推理使末端抖动降低 30%。 Combines torch.compile with 1-Rectified Flow to compress 10-step Eager denoising into 2 straight-line steps (258.8 ms to 9.7 ms loop P50; 415.9 ms to 157.3 ms full chunk P50), while step-adaptive RTC guidance (|Δt|·w ≤ 0.22) cuts few-step end-effector jerk by 30% vs. unguided inference.

能力边界：亚厘米窄槽竖直插书（盲测 2 / 15） Remaining Bottleneck: Sub-Centimeter Slot Insertion (2 / 15)

在桌面收纳盒前侧窄槽插书任务中，当机械臂夹持薄书移至隔槽上方时，书体遮挡了第三人称与腕部相机对隔板边缘的视线。纯视觉策略缺乏腕部力/触觉阻抗反馈，接触隔板边缘后易发生姿态偏转滑脱。 When positioning an upright book above the narrow front caddy compartment, the book body occludes the divider rim in both camera views. Without wrist force or tactile feedback, millimeter-scale rim contact triggers rotational slip.

本项工作的评测范围严格限定于固定 10 个目标任务家族在未见初始物理状态下的闭环迁移与源域保持，不作跨形态实机或任意未见新技能零样本泛化声明。 All reported gains reflect closed-loop transfer to unseen initial states across 10 target task families in MuJoCo simulation, without claiming real-robot deployment or arbitrary unseen-skill generalization.
